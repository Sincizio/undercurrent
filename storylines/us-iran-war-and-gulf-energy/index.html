US-Iran war and Gulf energy

Opened 11 November 2026 · Last update 13 November 2026

Summary

The US and Israel attacked Iran on 28 February 2026 and Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz on the first day. A June memorandum collapsed and strikes resumed; Qatar and Pakistan mediate. In the week to 10 November Washington signalled the war is winding down, with no settlement reported, crude fell to about $90, and linked theatres (Saudi-Houthi war, cyberattacks in Europe attributed by France to the IRGC) stayed active. Update 13 Nov: on 12 Nov Trump said the US no longer needs a deal with Iran and took the vice-president off the negotiations; Brent rebounded from a ten-week low of $91.5 to about $96. Iran says Hormuz will not return to its pre-war status; Israeli media report cabinet orders to prepare an attack.
