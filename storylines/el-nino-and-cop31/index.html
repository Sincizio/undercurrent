Super El Nino and COP31

Opened 11 November 2026 · Last update 13 November 2026

Summary

A very strong El Nino is developing: September 2026’s monthly Nino3.4 value was the highest on record and Carbon Brief’s analysis projects a peak around December near 4C, with the largest effect on global temperature in 2027. COP31 opened in Antalya on 9 November (to 20 November) with Australia presiding over negotiations and the US absent; the Global Carbon Budget projects a 0.4% fall in fossil CO2 in 2026 because of the oil shock.
