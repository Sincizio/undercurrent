Spain: general strike and snap election

Opened 13 November 2026 · Last update 13 November 2026

Summary

Spain held its first general strike in 14 years on 11 November 2026, called by the main unions over housing and the cost of living, 18 days before a snap general election on 29 November. Tens of thousands took part, there were clashes in Barcelona and the main container ports stopped or suspended operations. Rents and wages are central campaign issues.
