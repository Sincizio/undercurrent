AI build-out: politics, capital and capacity

Opened 11 November 2026 · Last update 13 November 2026

Summary

The AI investment boom met political and financial resistance in the first week of November 2026: voters rejected or limited data-centre development in 40 of 41 local measures, the midterm result raised the prospect of moratoria, Wall Street had its worst day since April 2025 on 5 November, and Anthropic set IPO terms below earlier expectations. Regulation is also tightening abroad (Australia). Memory scarcity shows the physical strain of the build-out.
