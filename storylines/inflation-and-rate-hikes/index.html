Energy-driven inflation and the return of rate rises

Opened 11 November 2026 · Last update 13 November 2026

Summary

The oil shock from the Iran war has pushed central banks back towards tightening. The Bank of England raised Bank Rate to 4% on 5 November; the Fed held at 3.75-4.00% on 28 October with two dissents for a rise, and October CPI plus Governor Waller’s 50bp remark sent the 10-year Treasury yield back to about 5% on 10 November. The ECB changes president in February 2027. Update 13 Nov: the 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5.05% on 12 Nov with a December Fed rise largely priced; the Fed revised its 2027 meeting schedule; UK third-quarter GDP grew 0.7%; US existing-home sales fell to their lowest since 2010 as mortgage rates passed 7%.
