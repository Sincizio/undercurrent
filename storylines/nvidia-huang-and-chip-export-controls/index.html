Nvidia, the Huang affair and chip export controls

Opened 11 November 2026 · Last update 13 November 2026

Summary

Nvidia’s board opened an independent investigation on 8 November 2026 into reports that its CEO was the target of a foreign intelligence effort; Jensen Huang admitted an affair. Reporting links the woman to Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer under US investigation for alleged chip diversion to China, which it denies. Congress is pushing export-control bills through the defence bill as Xi and Trump prepare to meet at APEC. Update 13 Nov: Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive and director on 12 Nov; finance chief Colette Kress is interim chief and Harvey C. Jones the first board chair. The shares lost more than 400 billion in value in the week (ANSA; currency not stated). Singapore barred the former Megaspeed executive from re-entry, and the commerce secretary’s position is reported to be under discussion.
